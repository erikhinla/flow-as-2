# Anti-Slop Protocol
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Authoritative Execution Standard  
**Source:** `The_Anti_Slop_Protocol.pdf`  
**Mode:** Applies to every creative export produced by FLOW Agent AS

Technical compliance is the floor. Impact is the requirement.

This text companion makes the visual protocol readable inside agent runtimes. The source PDF remains the visual authority. Where an example in the PDF appears to prescribe a permanent font, color, logo, token, or sound system, the Brand Bible's deliberately unlocked decisions control. Those examples are anti-default guidance, not identity locks.

## 1. Name the real problem

Digital Fog is the lived condition:

- Too many tabs, prompts, and inputs.
- Information feels everywhere and nowhere.
- Activity is high while progress is hard to feel.
- AI output creates more manual repair.

Digital Friction is the measurable business consequence:

- Projects stall while context is recovered.
- Handoffs fail between people, teams, and AI tools.
- Work is duplicated because the operating truth is missing.
- Essential knowledge depends on one person's memory.

Creative work must make this relationship visible. It may not collapse the ecosystem into a generic AI-product story.

## 2. Idea supremacy

Concept comes before compliance. Technical correctness is a failure when the artifact looks generated.

Materials, lighting, typography, interaction, and motion exist to strengthen the core idea. They are never the idea by themselves. Every asset must immediately answer: what is this trying to make someone think or feel?

If the emotional or intellectual objective is unclear, the work is incomplete.

## 3. Physical integrity

Digital work must feel built.

- Materials have mass.
- Light follows optical logic.
- Mechanical systems look engineered.
- Interfaces have hierarchy, edge behavior, and tactile response.
- Motion communicates weight, state, sequence, or consequence.

Surface polish without a defensible physical or conceptual logic is slop.

## 4. Declare the mode

Every creative task declares one primary mode before execution.

### Brand System Mode

Purpose: teach, explain, document, and clarify. Success is measured by clarity, accuracy, consistency, and legibility.

### Campaign Mode

Purpose: capture attention, create emotion, tell a story, and be remembered. Success is measured by memorability, emotion, storytelling, and craft.

A task that mixes both modes without naming the primary mode is defective.

## 5. Set the telemetry dials

Every campaign records three decisions:

- Design Variance: structural predictability from strict symmetry to aggressive asymmetry.
- Visual Density: either gallery-airy or cockpit-dense. Do not drift into an indecisive middle.
- Motion Intensity: from restrained to cinematic choreography.

High variance requires deliberate asymmetry, not random misalignment. High density requires exact hierarchy and monospace precision where data is real. High motion requires spring behavior, staggered orchestration, and physically plausible transitions.

Animate transform and opacity. Motion without narrative or feedback purpose is decoration.

## 6. Refuse generated defaults

The following are failure signals unless the concept provides a specific reason:

- Centered template heroes.
- Symmetrical three-card feature rows.
- Fake interface screenshots built from decorative boxes.
- Generic AI purple, neon gradients, and pure black fields.
- Inter, Times New Roman, Fraunces, or Instrument Serif used as automatic defaults.
- Oversaturated accents.
- Warm and cool gray systems mixed without intent.
- Repeated rounded cards, floating glass panels, and empty visual complexity.
- Typography or material choices that could be swapped into any SaaS page without changing the idea.

Typography and color are chosen for the concept. They are not permanently locked by this protocol.

## 7. Build campaign systems, not isolated images

A campaign proof must demonstrate one idea across a connected set of decisions. At minimum, when the brief asks for a campaign system, the package includes:

- A working primary web moment.
- A diagnostic or interaction state.
- A personalized result or conversion state.
- Desktop and mobile browser proof.
- A motion execution with intentional audio.
- Multiple platform-specific social placements.
- A copy and conversion map.

One generated image is not a campaign test.

## 8. Govern the execution

Governed work leaves durable residue:

1. Folders provide location, routing, and state.
2. Markdown records context, decisions, and instructions.
3. Scripts make execution repeatable and mechanically verifiable.
4. Protocols enforce rules, escalation, and human authority.

Generation is easy. Governance is hard. You cannot automate a mess.

## 9. Verification gates

Mechanical proof precedes human judgment.

### System validation

Verify files, dimensions, duration, audio, responsive states, routes, hashes, reduced-motion behavior, forbidden patterns, and `published: false`.

### Five-category agent gate

Every category must pass:

- Concept
- Craft
- Story
- Emotion / Curiosity
- Brand

One failure returns the work to revision.

### Human creative gate

The human decides whether the work is memorable, emotionally specific, audience-correct, and worthy of release. Mechanical proof and agent self-evaluation may stage the package for review. They may not publish it or declare it approved.

## 10. Authority loop

The operating sequence is:

1. Declare the creative mode.
2. Set the variance, density, and motion dials.
3. Enforce the anti-slop rules.
4. Execute the connected system.
5. Survive mechanical proof.
6. Survive the five-category agent gate.
7. Stop at documented human authority.
8. Capture what changed so the next execution improves.

Credibility is the residue of real, governed work.
