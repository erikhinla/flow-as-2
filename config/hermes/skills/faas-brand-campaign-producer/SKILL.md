# FAAS brand campaign producer

Use this skill when a FLOW Agent AS task is a creative export, campaign system, branded site moment, diagnostic, social placement, motion piece, or connected group of those assets.

## Authority order

1. The accepted task envelope and its output contract.
2. `TransformBy10X_Ecosystem_Brand_Bible_v1.0.md`.
3. `CREATIVE_EXECUTION_STANDARD.md`.
4. `ANTI_SLOP_PROTOCOL.md`.
5. Applicable product and copy Canon.

The open identity inventory is intentional. Do not invent a permanent logo system, fixed palette, font lock, icon family, token set, or sonic identity to make the task feel complete.

## Production sequence

1. Inspect every source before deciding whether it deserves a role.
2. State one memorable concept sentence.
3. Define the human or business contradiction that creates movement.
4. Decide how physical material, depth, scale, light, typography, motion, and sound express that idea.
5. Write finished public copy for every requested surface.
6. Reject familiar SaaS, AI-tech, and agency-template patterns explicitly.
7. Write the binding `creative-production-plan.json` requested by the runtime.
8. Let the repository renderer export those decisions reproducibly.
9. Inspect the actual files at desktop, mobile, social, and motion dimensions.
10. Revise or fail the work. Never turn a technical pass into a creative pass.

## Copy discipline

- TransformBy10X carries the master idea. Do not turn the full campaign into a BizBuilders AI or Fog-Lift campaign.
- Work and business consequences may route to BizBuilders AI. Personal Digital Fog may route to Fog-Lift.
- BizBot comes later, after the business is fixed enough that a bot will not make the mess worse.
- Headlines name a lived consequence in twelve words or fewer.
- Prefer pressure, recognition, and plain speech over categories and process language.
- Reject "handoff," "operational readiness," "operating layer," "transformation journey," "unlock," "leverage," "seamless," and similar corporate filler.
- B2B standard: "AI Created a Job. Nobody Wanted." and "Your AI Tools Didn't Remove the Work. They Reassigned It to You."

## Quality characteristics

- Sophisticated, not corporate.
- Precise, not decorative.
- Industrially weighted, not cold or generic.
- Technically clear and capable of campaign-level impact.
- One concept, expressed as a connected system rather than a pile of assets.
- Original footage may be evidence, texture, or story material. It is never mandatory hero imagery.
- No default logo header.
- No unexplained permanent identity decisions.
- No generic stock photography or image-model spectacle without narrative function.

## Completion evidence

A production run is valid only when the runtime evidence identifies the real owning agent, records the selected model and skill, hashes the binding plan, lists the exported files, passes deterministic validation, records independent reviews, and remains unpublished pending the human gate.
